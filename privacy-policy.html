Privacy Policy

Effective: August 29, 2026 · App: PH ICS FOG (com.knpn.phicsfog)

KNPN / Eric C. Colmenares (“we”, “us”) developed the PH ICS FOG mobile application (“the App”) as a free, offline reference guide to the Philippine Incident Command System Field Operations Guide.

No data is collected

The App is fully offline. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data with us or any third party. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

Checklists

Your checklist progress is stored only on your device (in the App’s local storage). It never leaves your device. The App’s “Clear Data” option removes only your checklist data.

Location (GPS)

The Tools → Geographic Location feature reads your device’s location only after you explicitly press Start and grant location permission. Coordinates are displayed on your device only; they are not stored and not transmitted anywhere. Tracking stops when you press Stop, leave the page, pause the App, or close it.

External links

Links (e.g. to Google Drive forms, the Google Play Store, or this policy) are opened in your device’s browser as user-initiated actions. Any site you open is governed by that site’s own privacy practices, not ours.

Permissions

The App requests only approximate and precise location permission for the optional GPS feature. It does not request Internet or background-location permission. Links you choose are handed to another app, such as your browser.

Children’s privacy

The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this address.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or the App, contact us at eccolmenares@gmail.com.
