COMMAND

Command refers jointly to the persons and functions of the IC and its command staff.

Guidelines for Command Staff

  1. Only one person will be designated for each position;
  2. Command Staff positions should not be combined;
  3. Command Staff positions may be filled by persons from other agencies or jurisdictions;
  4. Each of the positions may have assistants as necessary:
    1. Assistants are recommended & encouraged for larger incidents
    2. Assistants can be designated from other jurisdictions or agencies
  5. Command Staff members should report directly to the IC;
  6. Command Staff members may interact with any position within the ICS for purposes of information sharing; and
  7. Always exchange information.
Organizational Chart

Incident Commander

The Incident Commander (IC) is the individual responsible for all incident activities, including the development of strategies and tactics and the ordering and the release of resources. The IC has overall authority and responsibility for conducting incident operations and is responsible for the management of all incident operations at the incident site. The IC may use one or more deputies to perform specific tasks, reduce the IC’s span of control, or work in a relief capacity.

Safety Officer

The SOFR monitors incident operations and advises the IC / UC on all matters relating to operational safety, including the health and safety of emergency responder personnel. The SOFR has emergency authority of the IC / UC to stop or prevent unsafe acts during the incident operations.
