IMT Operating Guidelines

I. Mission Statement

The mission of the Incident Management Team (IMT) is, first and foremost, to provide safety for the responders and the public. The IMT shall provide the Responsible Official (RO) with organized, highly skilled and qualified personnel to respond to an incident within the jurisdiction or as authorized.

The IMT may also assist a requesting agency/authority to manage an incident located in another jurisdiction.  The requesting agency will maintain jurisdictional control of the incident with the support from the IMT through the following:

  • Provision of assistance for the protection of life, property and the environment;
  • Maintenance of the ICS standards and safety measures;
  • To coordinate multi-jurisdictional, multi-agency and multi-sectoral response; and
  • To provide efficient, less-costly and effective incident management services that will help mitigate the incident impacts and restore normalcy.
II. General Guidelines
  1. This guide is intended for use by the IMT members, ROs, and relevant DRRMC and agency personnel.
  2. The RO shall decide for the activation of ICS and designation of the IC who will organize the IMT.
  3. IMT status, availability, and mobilizations are coordinated through the DRRM Operations Center (OC)
  4. Regular IMT exercises shall be held to orient, inform, educate, and train team members. These exercises shall also support the development of team cohesion, and provide an opportunity to develop standard operating procedures for the team.
  5. ROs are invited to participate during these exercises. They may communicate any specific issues or concerns they have during their participation at the team meetings.
III. Expectations for IMT and Members
  1. The IMT shall ensure that safe operations are maintained during the entire incident, with special emphasis to safety during the transfer of command.
  2. The IMT shall meet mobilization criteria. e.g., maximum time allowed from initial activation to arrival at incident, using approved transportation, travel guidelines, etc.
  3. IMT assignments shall generally be up to 21 days, but may be extended.
  4. The hosting RO shall coordinate and interact with the Incident Commander (IC) and provide guidance utilizing through delegation of authority.
  5. The IMT shall be responsible for developing an Incident Action Plan.
  6. The IC shall immediately address the performance issues of the IMT members.
  7. Each IMT member shall initiate and maintain an ICS-214 form for the duration of the incident activation.  All significant and reportable actions shall be included on the form.
  8. All operational expenses such as tools, equipment, transportation and meals of responders shall be provided for the IMT and the responders using the Quick Response Fund of the DRRMC in charge.
  9. Each IMT member shall have access to the PPE and communication equipment.
  10. Each IMT member shall attend all appropriate team meetings and briefings.
  11. Each IMT member assigned as Command and General Staff shall function as a team player by being results-oriented, understanding the basis of the incident objectives, using a methodical and structured approach, seeking and sharing information freely, and recommending strategic adjustments for the team.
  12. Each IMT member shall stick to his/her designated roles.
  13. Each IMT member shall work as one to get the job done. All IMT members shall contribute meaningful and professional input to the team’s actions.
  14. Each IMT member shall maintain open and cooperative communications with the rest of the members. Each member shall prompt (vertical and horizontal) communications to subordinates, supervisors, and adjoining forces.
  15. Safety shall be one of the most important priorities of the IMT. Each IMT member shall be responsible for his/her and his/her team’s safety.
  16. Each IMT member shall recognize good, bad, and indifferent performance. Each must take immediate and positive measures to support good performance, correct sub-standard performance or inappropriate behavior.
  17. Each IMT member shall practice synergism. Synergism is the joint actions of all team members that, when taken together, increase each other’s effectiveness.  All problems are team problems.
  18. Each IMT member shall make contact and work with his/her counterpart. Each member shall show and practice sensitivity and prepare for team meetings (to contribute meaningfully to the achievement of tactical objective.) to get the initial command staff feelings and expectations. Leaders shall be wise enough to locate and utilize local talent of the members.
  19. Each IMT member shall be fit-for-duty at all times while deployed.
  20. At the conclusion of each incident, evaluation forms (ICS-225) shall be filled out by the appropriate command or general staff member for all team members or trainees who responded to the incident.
  21. The IMT shall be required to complete an Incident Narrative upon incident close-out and submit a copy of the narrative to the RO/DRRMC within two weeks of the closeout of the incident (see XIV. Incident Closeout).
  22. Team performance evaluations shall be based on the Performance Evaluation Form (see Appendix B) and be presented in conjunction with the Delegation of Authority.
  23. The IC shall complete an After Action Report with a Lessons Learned component.  The completed report will be sent to all IMT members and the DRRM OC.
  24. All pictures and videos shall be the property of the incident.  It is forbidden for members to copy, forward, reproduce or post any document to social media sites (e.g. Facebook, YouTube) without the permission of the agency having authority.
  25. Unauthorized activities and inappropriate behavior while on-duty such as the consumption of alcoholic beverages by IMT members shall strictly be prohibited.
IV. Selections and Qualifications
  1. All IMT members shall have completed the following ICS ladderized courses offered by the Office of Civil Defense (OCD):
    1. Three-Day Basic ICS Training Course (minimum requirement)
    2. Five-Day Integrated Planning Course on ICS
    3. Five-Day ICS Position Courses
    4. Five-Day All-Hazards Incident Management Team Training
  2. The RO shall select the appropriate IC for the incident.
  3. The selected IC shall select and organize the Command Staff and General Staff to constitute the IMT also based on the following qualifications:
    1. Level of knowledge on ICS based on the ladderized courses completed
    2. Appropriate skills, experience and expertise to handle similar incident
  4. Individuals shall be assigned to only one IMT at a time.
  5. No team member shall be supervised, either directly or at a higher level, by a relative or member of their household.
V. Agency Commitment to IMT Members
  1. The head of agency or local government unit (LGU), where the IMT members are officially designated at, shall commit to do the following:
    1. Ensure that the IMT members are available to respond to an incident upon request.
    2. Provide appropriate funding support, transportation, communication, team clothing/insignia (Uniform), and PPE, per diem, cash advance, and other administrative/logistical requirement to the IMT members.
    3. Provide insurance coverage/claims/additional compensation, benefits and other entitlements to the IMT members.
    4. Provide time and opportunity for the IMT members to attend mandatory ICS training, drills and emergency team activations.
    5. Issue an official memorandum/directive, which shall contain the following:
      1. Designation of the personnel as official IMT members
      2. Relief order from the IMT member’s primary position (similar to a jury duty) to be issued in his/her absence to allow for business continuity
      3. Authorization of the personnel (Department Order, Travel Mission Order, etc.) to respond to any incident as IMT member or as requested by the IC/RO
  2. The head of agency or local government unit (LGU) shall sign a Declaration of Commitment (Appendix 1), witnessed by the concerned OCD Regional Director, to ensure the proper implementation of ICS and activation of the IMT.
VI. IMT Composition
  1. The IMT shall consist of trained officers and personnel from the DRRMC member agencies, who have been sponsored by their respective agency heads and were officially trained by the OCD.
  2. The IMT shall generally be composed of the following positions: Incident Commander (IC), Deputy Incident Commander, Safety Officer (SO), Information Officer (IO), Liaison Officer (LO), Operations Section Chief (OSC), Planning Section Chief (PSC), Logistics Section Chief (LSC), and Finance/Administration Section Chief (FSC).
VII. IMT Activation Procedures
  1. The following criteria shall be observed for activating the appropriate IMT:         

    2. Directive from C, NDRRMC or ED, NDRRMC

    National IMT

    Directive from C, RDRRMC

    Regional IMT

    Directive from the Provincial Governor/ C, PDRRMC

    Provincial IMT

    Directive from the City/Municipal Mayor/ C,CDRRMC

    City/Municipal IMT
  2. The RO shall appoint and designate a personnel as the IC following the formal delegation of authority (Appendix 2).
  3. The IC shall select and organize his Command and General Staff to constitute the IMT.
  4. The IC shall work with the respective DRRM OC to notify IMT members and their immediate supervisor or head of office of their activation. The OC shall provide the following incident information:
    1. Type / nature / location of the incident / current situation
    2. Incident Command Post location (or location for the IMT to respond)
    3. Contact details of initial response/current IC
  5. The IMT members shall signify their availability upon the receipt of notification from the DRRM OC. The heads of the agencies of the IMT members shall allow the immediate deployment of the IMT for response.
  6. The DRRM OC shall acknowledge or confirm the IMT activation.
  7. The IMT members shall proceed to the reporting location within two (2) hours upon activation along with the written Department Orders, Travel Mission Orders, etc.
VIII. IMT Uniform and Insignia
  1. Each IMT member shall respond using a recognized uniform bearing the official Philippine ICS logo, name and IMT position.
  2. Command and General staff position vests shall be worn to assist in the IMT recognition.
  3. IMTs shall bear an insignia for distinction, in the event of multiple teams deployed in the area. The insignia will not include the names of vendors or products.
IX. Record-keeping and Documentation

The IMT of the respective agency DRRMC shall be responsible in maintaining the database of trained personnel who are certified to act as members of the IMT.  The database shall include, but not limited to, the following:

  1. Name, home address, and cellphone/ landline number and personal email address
  2. Agency, designation, work location, agency contact number and work email address
  3. Status (active or inactive)
  4. Certifications of Completion of ICS ladderized courses by OCD:
    1. Basic ICS
    2. Integrated Planning Course on ICS
    3. ICS Position Courses
    4. All Hazards Incident Management Team
  5. Applicable work experience and qualifications
  6. IMT Position/ Status
  7. Other relevant certifications
X. Replacement and Removal of IMT Members
  1. Any member of the IMT shall be replaced during duty due to sickness, injuries, sudden family emergencies, or for any valid reason as approved by the IC.
  2. In the case of the removal of the IC, the RO is authorized to replace the IC.
XI. Team Oversight
  1. The OCD office in charge of the area and the concerned DRRMC shall have the responsibility to manage, coordinate, oversee the implementation of ICS standards by the IMT.
  2. For disasters that are national in scope, the National IMT shall operate under Area Command to coordinate, supervise, monitor and report the implementation of tactical objectives by the Regional and Local IMTs.
XII. IMT Evaluation
  1. ROs/Agencies/DRRMCs shall utilize the standard IMT evaluation form (Appendix 3).
  2. The IC will submit a copy of the IC/IMT evaluation to ROs/Agencies/DRRMCs (see Team Expectations and Incident Closeout Sections) within two weeks of the closeout.
  3. Final evaluations will be reviewed by ROs/Agencies/DRRMCs to address performance issues and maintain consistency in the evaluation process.
XIII. Incident Closeout
  1. At the minimum, each IMT shall write a narrative and have a close out.
  2. The IMT shall provide an agenda and time frames for the close out to all agencies/departments that have been involved with the incident as decided by the IC and RO.
  3. Close out packages shall be made available to all relevant agencies/jurisdictions as determined by the IC and RO
