OPERATIONS SECTION

The Operations Section is responsible for managing tactical operations at the incident site to reduce immediate hazards, save lives and property, establish situation control and restore normal conditions.

Organizational Chart

Operations Section Chief (OSC)

The Operations Section Chief (OSC) activates and supervises elements in accordance with the Incident Action Plan (IAP) by:

  • Directing the execution of the IAP
  • Activating and executing the Site
  • Safety and Health Plan
  • Directing and preparing the unit operational plans
  • Requesting or releasing resources
  • Making expedient changes to the IAPs, as necessary
  • Reporting to the IC / UC
  • May assign a deputy OSC to assist in supervising operations activities
